Privacy Policy — Islamic Life Companion

1. Overview

Islamic Life Companion is an offline-first Islamic utility and educational library application providing prayer times, Qibla compass, Quran reader, Kutub al-Sittah Hadith library, Adhkar, Tasbih, Hijri calendar, Qaza-e-Umer tracker, fasting times, and an optional Islamic research assistant.

2. Location Permission & GPS Usage

  • Purpose: When enabled by you after reviewing our in-app disclosure, the app accesses device location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to compute local Islamic prayer timings (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), fasting times (Sehri, Iftar), and Qibla direction toward the Holy Kaaba.
  • On-Device Processing: All astronomical prayer calculations (adhan library) and Qibla calculations run 100% locally on your device.
  • Storage: Your coordinates and timezone are saved exclusively on your device inside local storage (islamic_app_settings_v1) and are never uploaded to developer-owned servers.
  • Opt-Out: You may decline GPS permission at any time and choose a city manually or enter custom coordinates in Settings.

3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following preferences and worship logs are stored exclusively on your device via WebView localStorage and IndexedDB and are never uploaded to developer servers:

  • Language selection (Urdu, Arabic, English, Hindi).
  • Selected city, calculation method, juristic method (Hanafi / Shafi), Hijri date adjustment, prayer offsets, and display/font settings.
  • Quran bookmarks and last-read Surah/Ayah position.
  • Downloaded Surahs (IslamicLifeCompanionQuranDB) and downloaded Hadith collections/bookmarks (IslamicLifeCompanionHadithDB).
  • Qaza-e-Umer missed prayer counters (islamic_app_qaza_records_v1).
  • Daily Adhkar counts, Tasbih counters, custom Dhikr presets, Tasbih history, daily Ayah/Hadith rotation state, and local research history (IslamicLifeCompanionAssistantDB).

4. Quran & Hadith Downloads, Audio Streaming & Third-Party Services

Built-in Azan recordings, prayer calculations, starter Surahs, and seed Hadiths work offline. When you download full collections, stream Ayah audio, or load web fonts while online, standard HTTPS requests (cleartext: false) are sent to public content providers:

  • Quran Arabic & Urdu Text: https://api.alquran.cloud/v1
  • Quran Ayah Audio Streaming: https://cdn.islamic.network/quran/audio/
  • Hadith Collections (Kutub al-Sittah): https://cdn.jsdelivr.net and https://raw.githubusercontent.com (fawazahmed0/hadith-api)
  • Typography / Web Fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com and https://fonts.gstatic.com

When your device connects to these third-party providers, they may process standard connection metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) under their own privacy policies.

5. Optional AI Research Assistant

  • Default Offline Mode: By default, the Islamic Research Assistant operates locally on your device using available Quran and Hadith texts. No questions leave your device in offline mode.
  • Optional Online AI Mode: Only if you explicitly enable "Online AI Reasoning" and connect to an active configured backend AI endpoint (/api/ai-research) are your typed question and retrieved local excerpts processed via the Google Gemini API (@google/genai) to generate an evidence-grounded response.

6. Device Sensors, Vibration & Notifications

  • Orientation Sensor: Used locally via DeviceOrientationEvent to rotate the Qibla compass dial; sensor data is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used only for optional tactile feedback on Tasbih, Adhkar, and Qibla alignment.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) & Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Used only when enabled in Settings to display local prayer alerts while the app is open in the foreground and keep screen/playback active during Azan recitation.

7. Future Advertising & User Consent (Applicable Only If Enabled Later)

This version of Islamic Life Companion contains no advertisements, does not integrate Google AdMob, and does not collect or share Advertising IDs. If Google AdMob is added in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated beforehand, and required consent dialogs (such as Google UMP for EEA/UK users) will be shown prior to ad personalization.

8. Data Deletion & Your Privacy Rights

Because Islamic Life Companion does not create user accounts or store personal profiles on developer servers, you have complete local control over your data. You can reset preferences or clear downloaded Hadiths, Tasbih history, Qaza records, and research history inside the app, or remove all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Islamic Life Companion → Storage → Clear data or by uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Islamic Life Companion does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect new features or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this hosted Privacy Policy page and reflected inside the application.
